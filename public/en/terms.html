Terms & Privacy

Wish Tree is a shared digital public board where anyone can hang a wish, watch a cherry blossom bloom, and share positivity with visitors worldwide.

1. Privacy & Data Collection

We believe in absolute simplicity and privacy. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or user accounts. When you hang a wish, only the text you write, an optional nickname, and the creation timestamp are stored in our secure database.

2. Community Guidelines & Moderation

Because all wishes are publicly visible to everyone on the tree, wishes pass through automated content safety filtering (local filters and NVIDIA NIM AI guardrails) before appearing on the tree. Hate speech, harassment, profanity, spam, and harmful content are automatically rejected.

3. Spam Prevention

We use hCaptcha solely to verify that wish submissions are made by human visitors, protecting the tree from spam bots.
